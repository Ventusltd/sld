# Public project references for drawing review

These are links for engineering study. No open redistribution licence was
established; the documents are not mirrored here. Neither source certifies our
symbols or supplies the complete approved-drawing evidence required for production.

## Recent large proposed UK network

Welsh Government Bro Tathan Power market-engagement document, March 2026:
https://www.brotathan.wales/s/50101-XX-XX-T-P-30501-Bro-Tathan-Power-MOI-090326.pdf

Appendix A1, PDF page 18: Roadnight Taylor's **132kV Final Arrangement from
Aberthaw B SLD**, revision R6, 17 November 2025, sheet 1 of 1, A3. The network
proposal is up to 280 MVA. The sheet shows main/reserve busbars, incoming circuits,
couplers, customer branches, metering, future bays and ownership distinctions.
It is explicitly preliminary/provisional, subject to studies. It is not approved
for construction; explicit IEC 60617 conformity was not established.

Official project source: https://www.brotathan.wales/news

## Built UK installation with documented standards basis

National Grid Electricity Distribution Take Charge closedown report, May 2023:
https://commercial.nationalgrid.co.uk/downloads-view-reciteme/635097

Figure 4, printed page 11 / PDF page 12, is the Compact Connection Solution SLD:
two 33 kV incomers, primary busbar, breaker, 33/11.5 kV transformer and outgoing
feeders. The installed solution is 12 MVA and was energized in June 2022. This
figure is a functional-specification extract, not itself labelled AFC/as-built.

The separate as-built specification, V1 of 31 May 2023, references BS EN 61936-1,
BS EN 60076 and BS EN 62271, but no IEC 60617 symbol-conformance claim was found:
https://commercial.nationalgrid.co.uk/downloads-view-reciteme/634074

The latter endpoint may refuse automated downloads; do not bypass access controls.
Official project page: https://commercial.nationalgrid.co.uk/innovation/projects/take-charge

## Proposed comparisons

Record equipment class, electrical terminal count, symbol state, busbar grouping,
voltage, rating, ownership boundary, drawing identity/revision, and evidence
location. Review breaker versus disconnector versus earthing-switch semantics.
Keep unavailable or uncertain data explicit. Compare a source's defined scope,
not visual similarity alone. Only a reviewed and appropriately licensed reference
can become a production acceptance fixture.
