03 / Learn before you draw

One line, many conductors

An SLD abstracts a power system into equipment and electrical connections. A block diagram shows functional relationships and does not establish electrical connectivity.

A terminal is not an anchor

Terminals belong to electrical equipment. Anchors help a renderer attach lines. Several anchors may represent alternative approaches to one terminal.

Open does not mean approved

A licence describes reuse rights. Symbol conformity needs separate engineering evidence, a drawing reference and a reviewer. Read the drawing guide →
